       Welcome to Visionary Innovation Hub

Innovation Hub is a dynamic and innovative center dedicated to fostering high-quality research and development opportunities for entrepreneurs and innovators in Hong Kong and beyond. Established in 2015, our mission is to create a collaborative environment that supports cutting-edge technology and sustainable practices across various sectors.

We have over 100,000 square feet, strategically located in Kowloon's High-Tech Park, where we comfortably host 150 local innovators, 370 spin-off companies, and 50+ research groups. Visionary Innovation Hub is home to start-ups, researchers, industry leaders, and educational institutions.

We emphasize a student-centered approach by encouraging undergraduate students to engage in research and development activities from their first year. Our priority is collaboration with academia and the private sector, helping talented participants transform technological ideas into successful products, digital platforms, or services with real-world application. We are proud to partner with leading universities, including Harvard University and MIT.

For society and industry, we ensure recognized leaders in their respective fields provide students with guidance and experience through hands-on research within the Technology Zone, Innovation Zone, and Ecology Zone. Each area has created a unique perspective of focus on research, innovation, and sustainable development of the next generation of technology, along with a positive impact on society.

    Visionary Innovation Hub was highly recognized in 2024 when it was awarded as one of the Top 50 Hubs worldwide, excelling in academic collaborations, industrial alliances, and nurturing future leaders. The Visionary Innovation Hub is your platform for innovation and growth.
